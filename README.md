# Marketing Analytics and Customer Segmentation Using Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing customer segmentation data using Microsoft Excel to understand customer characteristics, spending behaviour, and demographic patterns.

Developed as part of my MBA Marketing coursework, this project combines data analysis, visualisation, customer segmentation, and marketing strategy development to demonstrate how customer insights can support targeted marketing campaigns.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyse customer characteristics across different demographic groups.
* Explore spending patterns across customer segments.
* Examine relationships between age, gender, occupation, and spending score.
* Identify potential target audiences for marketing campaigns.
* Develop segment-specific campaign themes based on customer characteristics.
* Apply data-driven insights to marketing decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

**Microsoft Excel**

Techniques applied:

* Data Analysis and Organisation
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts and Data Visualisation
* Customer Segmentation
* Demographic Analysis
* Spending Behaviour Analysis
* Marketing Campaign Planning

## 📊 Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Demographic Analysis

Examined customer characteristics, including age, gender, marital status, and family size, to understand the composition of different customer groups.

### 2. Occupation-Based Analysis

Explored customer distribution across professional categories to identify potential differences in customer characteristics.

### 3. Spending Score Analysis

Analysed spending score categories to understand variations in customer spending behaviour.

### 4. Age-Based Customer Segmentation

Examined customer groups across different age categories to identify potential opportunities for age-specific marketing strategies.

### 5. Gender and Customer Segment Analysis

Compared customer characteristics across genders and segments to explore opportunities for more relevant marketing communication.

## 💡 Marketing Strategy Development

An important part of this project was translating data analysis into potential marketing campaign ideas.

The analysis considered customer characteristics such as:

* Age group
* Occupation
* Spending score
* Gender

These attributes were used to develop campaign themes for selected customer segments.

The objective was to demonstrate how customer segmentation can help marketers design more relevant promotional messages and improve audience targeting.
